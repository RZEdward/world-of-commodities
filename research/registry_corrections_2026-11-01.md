## Tokyo living-cost estimate

For the University of Tokyo course, the previous living-cost field said `Estimated - Not confirmed`, so the dashboard suppressed it as missing. Replaced it with approximately USD 9,900/year, derived from UTokyo's published ¥130,000/month student living-cost guidance and ECB 30 October 2026 reference rates (USD 1.1172 / EUR; JPY 175.68 / EUR). The two-year total now includes tuition/admission plus estimated living costs (~USD 28,400), excluding application, travel and arrival expenses.

Sources:
- https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/content/400262143.pdf
- https://t-cens.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/guidance_information/?id=expenses
- https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/index.hr.html
