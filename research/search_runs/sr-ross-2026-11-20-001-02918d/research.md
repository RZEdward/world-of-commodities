# Focused job sweep — Singapore, Hong Kong and Tier 1 employers

**Run date:** 20 November 2026 (Dubai time)  
**Owner:** Ross  
**Scope:** Broad vacancy discovery in registered Singapore and Hong Kong, plus global vacancy-inventory checks for company-ranking positions 1–122. This run did not cover all 14 city-led searches, all 438 ranked companies, or university programmes.

## Coverage and limits

- Singapore and Hong Kong received city-led vacancy discovery for energy, power, gas, commodities, market fundamentals, quantitative analysis and adjacent analyst titles. Broad title-led searches in the other 12 registered job-search cities are deferred.
- All 122 Tier 1 ranked companies (ranks 1–36 Core priorities and 37–122 Power & Gas specialists) have an explicit outcome in `employer-outcomes.json`. Four companies are marked `source_unavailable`; these are not treated as no-vacancy results. Ranks 123–438 were not searched in this focused run.
- Employer inventories and public job searches were checked with official career/ATS sources first. LinkedIn and aggregators are retained as labeled leads where the employer ATS posting could not be verified.
- No university/course sweep was included. Vacancy review counts and tool-call totals were not consistently captured, so they are left blank/not surfaced rather than estimated as facts. Elapsed wall time is approximate from run preparation to close.
- City-only postings are pinned to their registered city coordinate. No street-office address was invented. Application and interview statuses were left unchanged.
