# Masters course additions — 2026-11-06

## Scope and outcome

Targeted course-discovery delta following Ross's request to include programmes explicitly named around commodities and strongly related energy engineering/markets courses. New entries are restricted to existing University Search cities, except Geneva was explicitly enabled in the Location Registry because the University of Geneva's exact-title MSc in Commodity Trading is a direct match. Search additions: 5 programmes, 4 new university registry rows, 4 new campus rows. Existing HKU entry/campus was reused. These five additions are not a claim that every programme across all 35 registered universities was rechecked in this batch.

## Programmes added

- University of Geneva — Master of Science in Commodity Trading. Sept 2027 entry; current admissions page states 15 Jan–28 Feb 2027 application window, GMAT or GRE mandatory, one-page English motivation letter, and Swiss commodity-industry internship condition. 1.5 years / 90 ECTS; official curriculum includes commodity price mechanisms, oil/gas, renewables, agriculture, metals, shipping, hedging, data science and fundamental analysis. Tuition CHF500 per semester; Geneva living budget estimated CHF1,800–1,900/month. Uni Mail campus coordinates from named OSM campus relation, matched to the official building address.
- HKUST — MSc Chemical and Energy Engineering. Sept 2027; 1 Dec 2026 Round 1. One year full-time, HKD229,000 fee; relevant chemical/energy engineering or related-discipline bachelor required. Course fit is constrained by its chemical/materials engineering emphasis and Ross's degree eligibility must be checked.
- HKU — MSc(Eng) in Energy Engineering. Sept 2027; non-local main deadline 4 Jan 2027. One year full-time; proposed non-local tuition HKD395,000 subject to approval; engineering or related-science background required. Power systems/energy engineering focus.
- PolyU — Master of Science in Sustainable Energy. Sept 2027; main-round deadline 25 Feb 2027 (early round has passed). 1.5 years full-time; HKD9,000/credit; 31 credits with one required ethics credit not charged. Includes renewable power, energy storage, energy/climate, and optional AI/machine-learning coursework.
- CityUHK — MSc Energy and Environment. Sept 2027; 31 Mar 2027 deadline, rolling review. One year full-time; 30 credits at HKD7,100 each. Asian energy/environment economics, policy, law and technical science; physical-science/engineering entry route.

Tuition and living-cost USD figures are approximate conversions/planning estimates; the workbook notes distinguish official fees from living estimates. New university-wide reputation scores are based on QS World University Rankings 2027 positions (#168 Geneva, #33 HKUST, #50 PolyU, #52 CityUHK). Programme quality and Ross's course fit are scored separately.
