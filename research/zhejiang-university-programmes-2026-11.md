## 2027 Embodied Intelligence and Robotics (Hangzhou)

The official 2027 project page describes a full-time engineering master’s project in Hangzhou. Published curriculum themes include robotics, reinforcement learning, VLA models, embodied mobility/manipulation, multimodal perception and control; the project has industry practice and partnerships including Deep Robotics. The supervisor list gives direct contacts, including Qiu Guo Zhu's team (qgzhu@zju.edu.cn), Jun Wu's team (junwuapc@zju.edu.cn), and admissions contact gcsxyzs@zju.edu.cn.

The 2027 Engineer College prospectus states a 3-year duration and CNY 40,000 tuition for the full programme. Split evenly and converted using ECB 2026-10-30 reference rates, that is about USD 2,005/year. ZJU's published guidance and dorm rates support an estimate of about USD 6,315/year for living and a shared dorm; access and actual personal costs may differ.

The entry follows China's national postgraduate entrance examination. Registration ran Oct 15–24, 2026 and has passed. The project page does not confirm international-student eligibility or language of instruction. The Masters row is **On Hold**, not an open application. For the next cycle, ask admissions whether non-Chinese applicants may apply, whether the national exam and Chinese-language ability are mandatory, and confirm prerequisites, current fees, campus and dates.

## 2027 Intelligent Flight Technology and Equipment (Control Engineering)

This is a second Hangzhou project relevant to the user's interest in robotics and intelligent systems. The project covers intelligent perception/control for unmanned air and sea systems, autonomous navigation, human–machine cooperation, multi-system coordination and planning, machine learning and visual perception. The published 2027 plan allocates 10 places to Control Engineering and includes enterprise engineering practice.

The general 2027 prospectus states a 3-year duration and CNY 40,000 total tuition for standard engineering projects. The same ECB conversion gives about USD 2,005/year; the workbook carries the same clearly labelled, medium-low-confidence Hangzhou living estimate. Its 2027 exam-registration window also closed Oct 24. International eligibility and instruction language are not stated, so this separate Masters record is also **On Hold**. For a future cycle, ask admissions whether overseas applicants qualify, which language/exam route applies, and which supervisors accept new students.
